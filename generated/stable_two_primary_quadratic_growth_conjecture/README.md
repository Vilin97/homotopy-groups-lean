# `stable_two_primary_quadratic_growth_conjecture`

Quadratic logarithmic growth of cumulative two-primary stable stems

- Problem ID: `stable_two_primary_quadratic_growth_conjecture`
- Test Problem: no
- Submitter: OpenAI Codex
- Notes: knowledge_status=open_conjecture_in_cited_source; Conjecture 1.4. The two-primary order is the cardinality of elements killed by a power of two in the stable representative pi_(2k+2)(S^(k+2)); its cumulative product from stem 1 through k has log base two divided by k squared tending to a nonzero real constant.
